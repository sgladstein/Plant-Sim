## Review follow-ups (fold into a later PR)
- WP21 review (/mnt/project-files/reviews/wp21-review.md), still open after WP24:
  - Density is still short (-43% against -70%). Branches and leaf size do not respond to density, and stand LAI is 5.5 at 34 per m² against a real plateau of 3 to 4. The lever is bud outgrowth and leaf potential area scaling with the R:FR shade signal. Read the density variables from interior plants, not edge plants (a plant filter on the target).
  - Plot sides that are not multiples of 5 cm are rounded silently: reject them in `validate`, or document the rounding.
  - The golden mismatch text should say the golden holds 30 days.
  - Per-column soil surface radiation before WP26.
  - light.md: the plot grid bottom is `min(0, lowest leaf)`. world-and-schedule: list the first plant's G as a plot parameter.
  - Compute the light inputs and leaflet list once a day. Settle the G question in decision 0018.
  - Light model, no self-shading within a cell (found in the WP22 review). In ps-light each leaf's rays start at its own center, so leaves that share a voxel or a 3 cm layer never shade each other. A flat mat one voxel thick is invisible to the tracer, while a vertical column is not. That is why 8 fallen vines (LAI 2 to 3 over the heaps) show no self-shading, and it fits the WP12 finding that the model is 10% too bright. Fix: a half-cell start term in `coefficients()`, which multiplies a leaf's transmittance by exp(-G x (its cell's LAD minus its own) x half the cell path). It changes every golden, so it needs a model bump. Acceptance: run the two ray-tracer fixtures before and after. The lodged-row response would then gain a light share.
- WP25 (decision 0024), for later packages:
  - A topped plant ends with fewer branches than an untopped one: each released bud makes one 3-node branch with no buds of its own. Branches on branches, and a branch taking over as leader after topping, would make topping pay off as it does in a garden.
  - Seed library lots record count and mass; WP29 adds each seed's parents and genotype; WP30 sows saved seeds (`SeedChoice::Saved`) and keeps a library across worlds (`ps-breed`).
  - Since WP29, a replay of a pre-WP29 log that harvests gives a new hash under the same model version, because seed lots now carry `seed_genotypes`. Old saves still load and keep their stored hashes.
  - The sample weather file is synthetic (network policy blocked the open archives); replace it with a station file with a clear licence.
  - Perf (perf thread, a12d5aa): wheat_stand costs 111 ms per sim-day on 4 cores (light 48%); levers are fewer rays per leaf or one light sample per tiller, both model choices.
  - Review of WP25 (/mnt/project-files/reviews/wp25-review.md).
